# 🌐 AMC Custom Domain & Google Indexing Guide

El dafeel el kamel le-rabt el Domain `amc-eg.net` 3ala Vercel w thzoor el site fe Google Search.

---

## 1️⃣ Khotwa 1: Rabt el Domain 3ala Vercel (Vercel Domain Setup)

1. Edkhol 3ala **[Vercel Dashboard](https://vercel.com)** ➔ Project **`amc`**.
2. Edkhol 3ala **Settings** ➔ **Domains**.
3. Fe khanet **Add Domain**:
   - Ektb: `amc-eg.net` w edos **Add**.
   - Ekhtar **Add Both** (`amc-eg.net` & `www.amc-eg.net`).
4. Fe el site elly eshtret menoh el domain (zay Namecheap / GoDaddy):
   - **A Record**: 
     - **Name:** `@`
     - **Value / IP:** `76.76.21.21`
   - **CNAME Record**: 
     - **Name:** `www`
     - **Value:** `cname.vercel-dns.com`

---

## 2️⃣ Khotwa 2: Indexing 3ala Google (Google Search Console)

1. Edkhol 3ala **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)**.
2. Deef el Domain bta3k: `amc-eg.net`.
3. E3mel **Request Indexing** 3shan Google y-index el site fe netayeeg el search lma 7ad yktb **"AMC"**.

---

## 3️⃣ Khotwa 3: Google Business Profile (Google Maps)

1. Edkhol 3ala **[Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/)**.
2. E3mel Profile b-esm: **AMC Medical Equipment** (أو **AMC Egypt**).
3. Deef el Website URL: `https://www.amc-eg.net` w el رقم w el 3enwan.
4. Awwal ma Google yf3el el profile, lma 7ad yktb "AMC" hytl3lo karten kbeer fe Google Search w 3ala Google Maps!
